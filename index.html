About

HARWIS is a desktop assistant built and operated by an independent developer. It runs locally on the owner's PC (local language models, local files) and helps with everyday routine: organising documents, answering messages, and preparing short-form video content and small games.

The project is not a public social network, not a marketplace and not a data broker. It has no public user accounts: every installation serves its owner only.

Publishing to TikTok and Instagram

  1. The assistant prepares a video (a gameplay clip, a trailer, a short explainer).
  2. It shows the video and the proposed caption to the owner and asks for confirmation.
  3. Only after the owner explicitly says "yes" is the video sent to the owner's own account through the official TikTok Content Posting API or the Instagram API.
  4. The owner can disconnect the account at any moment from the app or from the TikTok / Instagram settings.

Nothing is ever posted automatically, on behalf of other people, or to accounts the owner does not control.
