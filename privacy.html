1. Who we are

HARWIS is a personal desktop assistant operated by an independent developer (the "owner"). Each installation runs on the owner's own computer and serves the owner only. The app has no public registration and no shared user database.

2. What data the app handles

  • Login tokens for the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts, obtained through the official OAuth login of each platform. They are stored encrypted on the owner's computer (Windows DPAPI) and are never sent to us or to any third party other than the platform that issued them.
  • Basic profile information of that account (display name, avatar, posting limits) that the platform returns so the app can show the connected account and check whether posting is possible.
  • Videos and captions that the owner chose to publish. They are sent to the platform at the moment of publishing, after the owner's explicit confirmation.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data.
  • We do not collect data about other TikTok or Instagram users.
  • We do not post anything automatically or on behalf of third parties.
  • We do not use platform data for advertising, profiling or to train AI models.

4. Where data lives

All data stays on the owner's computer. This website is static: it has no forms, no analytics and no cookies, and does not collect visitor data. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) under their own policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account is connected. The owner can disconnect the account at any time in the app (this deletes the stored tokens) and can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services, or in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. To ask for any related data to be deleted, use the contact below.

6. Third-party platforms

When the owner connects an account, the platform's own terms and privacy policy apply to the data held by that platform: TikTok, Instagram.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

To ask a question or request deletion of related data, open an issue on GitHub.
